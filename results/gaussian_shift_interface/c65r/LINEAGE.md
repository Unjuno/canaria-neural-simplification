# C65R lineage and claim boundary

C65R belongs to the repository Residual-MLP sequence C61R → C62R → C63R → C64R → C65R.

- C62R was exploratory and selected QR-P2.
- C63R used a separate fresh cohort and prospectively confirmed P2 non-inferiority to P4 under its locked margins.
- C64R prospectively explored P0/P1/P2 and selected P0 via a pre-locked smallest-first decision tree (`ADVANCE_P0_TO_C65R`).
- C65R used another separate fresh cohort and a P0-versus-P2-only confirmatory design with no P1 rescue.
- C65R terminal decision: **`C65R_CONFIRMATORY_PASS`**.

P0 is the directly compiled frozen recursive hierarchy: no teacher-residual correction and no top-boundary adaptation. A C65R PASS means this P0 condition met the preregistered non-inferiority criteria relative to P2 under the exact C65R protocol. It does not imply zero teacher residual, representation identity, universal irrelevance of teacher correction, or a universal zero-dimensional interface.

The C61R–C65R sequence is a **Residual-MLP** evidence line. It is not a continuation or confirmation of the imported **Residual CNN** C59/C60 experiments. Cross-architecture claims require a controlled protocol in which architecture is the intended varying factor rather than being confounded with boundary, representation distribution, replacement grammar, or calibration regime.
