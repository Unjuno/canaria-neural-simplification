# C64R lineage and publication boundary

C64R follows the repository Residual-MLP sequence C61R → C62R → C63R.

- C62R was exploratory and selected QR-P2.
- C63R used a separate fresh cohort and prospectively confirmed P2 non-inferiority to P4 under its locked margins.
- C64R is a new prospective **exploratory** frontier experiment testing P0 and P1 against that confirmed P2 reference.
- C64R selected P0 via the prospectively locked decision tree, producing `ADVANCE_P0_TO_C65R`.

`ADVANCE_P0_TO_C65R` is **not** a P0 sufficiency claim. P0 requires a separate fresh prospective confirmation before it can support a non-inferiority statement.

P0 means the frozen recursive hierarchy is compiled directly, with no teacher-residual correction and no top-boundary adaptation. It does not mean that the teacher residual is zero, that internal representations are identical, or that teacher information is universally unnecessary.

The C61R–C64R sequence is a Residual-MLP evidence line. It is not a continuation or confirmation of the imported Residual CNN C59/C60 experiments. Do not combine their interface dimensions into an architecture-wide complexity ordering without a controlled cross-architecture protocol.
