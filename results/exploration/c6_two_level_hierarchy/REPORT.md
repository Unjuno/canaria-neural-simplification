# C6 two-level hierarchical Canaria recompilation — exploratory report

Decision: **EXPLORATORY POSITIVE**. This is not confirmatory evidence.

C6 tests a second recursive level while forbidding the original four-block teacher output as a Level-2 fitting target. The hierarchy is exactly budget matched at every aggregate level: four local `TinyRes(64,8)` units total 4096 learned parameters, two recursively generated `TinyRes(64,16)` pair units total 4096, and the final `TinyRes(64,32)` contains 4096.

Fresh exploratory seeds: `1370–1372`; all three teachers passed the locked validation-accuracy eligibility rule. Held-out test data was not evaluated.

## Main result

The lower-level adapted Canaria IR already had mean validation NMSE **0.06427** versus the original teacher. Recompiling its two pair spans independently into `C12` and `C34`, then composing them, introduced additional error. A Level-2 joint adaptation of `[C12,C34]` using only the lower-level Canaria IR as target reduced that error in all three seeds.

| condition | mean validation NMSE vs original |
|---|---:|
| lower-level composed Canaria IR | 0.06427 |
| C12+C34 before Level-2 adaptation | 0.06786 |
| C12+C34 after Canaria-only Level-2 joint adaptation | 0.06287 |
| final depth-two single, no Level-2 adaptation | 0.06987 |
| **final depth-two single, Level-2 joint adaptation** | **0.06602** |
| flat single compiled directly from lower Canaria IR | 0.06489 |
| direct single compiled from original teacher | 0.03900 |

Level-2 joint adaptation improved final NMSE relative to no Level-2 adaptation in **3/3 seeds**, by `-0.00594`, `-0.00271`, and `-0.00290`.

The final depth-two single versus the flat single compiled directly from the same lower-level Canaria IR had NMSE ratios:

- seed 1370: **1.0070x**
- seed 1371: **1.0186x**
- seed 1372: **1.0269x**

Mean ratio: **1.0175x**.

Thus, in this exploratory setup, the extra hierarchy level itself added only about 1–3% functional NMSE relative to a flat compilation from the same lower-level IR.

## Critical boundary

Direct original-teacher compilation remained substantially better. The depth-two/direct-original ratios were **1.6975x, 1.7242x, 1.6548x**. C6 therefore does not show lossless hierarchy. Instead it suggests that most of the final error is inherited from the lower-level Canaria IR rather than generated by the second recursive level.

The next step is a fresh confirmatory cohort with prospectively fixed bounds on (1) Level-2 joint-adaptation benefit and (2) incremental depth-two cost relative to flat compilation from the same lower-level IR.
