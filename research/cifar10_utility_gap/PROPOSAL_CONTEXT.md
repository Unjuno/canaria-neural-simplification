## Hypothesis and fixed design

**Hypothesis:** within the fixed two-map joint replacement family, extra optimization, width, and task-aligned supervision may make distinguishable contributions. They need not be mutually exclusive. This is a mechanistic exploratory follow-on, not a rescue or retest of the original utility claim.

Use **Sep-joint architecture only**, for a controlled width/objective study—not because it wins validation (it does not). Holding its two residual maps, activations, 5×5 maximum receptive field and span fixed avoids the Comp/Sep architecture confound. Scope is explicitly this family; no inference that an answer also applies to Comp or Sep-local. Do not change this choice after teacher 03 or official-test outcomes.

1. **Data:** within the existing 5,000 calibration IDs, freeze a fresh 4,000-fit/1,000-diagnostic partition. Sort IDs by SHA256 of UTF-8 `canaria-utility-gap-v1:<decimal ID>` (ID tie-break); first 1,000 are diagnostic. No class-label stratification, candidate splits, or partition search. Publish exact IDs and hashes before fitting. Original teachertrain and normalization remain unchanged. Never read the original 5,000 validation images or their labels in this follow-on; never read official test. Fresh students are essential: original trained replacement weights have already seen every diagnostic image, so **no warm starts or continuation from pilot students**. This is a holdout only for the fresh 4,000-fit students: those 1,000 images were used by the initial pilot fits, and this family/design is being proposed adaptively after pilot outcomes. It is **not a pristine study-level holdout or independent generalization/selection evidence**, even though the teacher itself did not train on calibration.
2. **Cohort:** all planned teachers 26102601/02/03, only after original cohort lock and the existing teacher-quality admission. No substitutes. A failed original teacher remains an explicit not-admitted cell; do not reinterpret a smaller cohort as n=3.
3. **Factorial cells:** (a) two maps with ranks [8,8], 5,120 weights, or [16,16], 10,240 weights; crossed with (b) common-endpoint MSE, or pure teacher-logit distillation KL. Both widths retain the same depth, activations, residual additions and receptive field. No biases, BN, repair, or additional trainable parameters.
4. **Initialization:** each width constructed afresh in its own isolated Torch RNG seeded by the teacher seed, using the existing default Conv2d initialization. Each objective receives an exact deep copy of that width's initial state. Persist hashes before fitting. Do not claim initialization equivalence across widths.
5. **Objectives:** MSE is `mean((student_endpoint - teacher_endpoint)^2)`. KL is per-example `sum_c q_teacher[c] * (log(q_teacher[c]) - log(q_student[c]))`, averaged across examples, with softmax temperature exactly 1; use stable log-softmax. No loss mixture, temperature search, or labels in training. Teacher targets are detached. For student logits, backpropagate through the frozen suffix to replacement weights; teacher parameters and all BN buffers remain frozen EVAL. **Existing `installed_logits` has `@torch.no_grad()` and cannot implement this training path.** New separately reviewed code must retain the input gradient without updating the suffix.
6. **Schedule:** one fixed run to **2,048 updates** per cell, batch128 with the final32 retained: 32 updates/pass, 64 complete passes of 4,000 IDs. Freeze checkpoints at **1,024 and 2,048** updates (32/64 passes). Same seed-specific immutable permutation/batch sequence across all four cells; use the existing Adam recipe unchanged (lr1e-3, betas .9/.999, eps1e-8, no decay/clipping/schedule). Fresh optimizer state. No early stopping, rerun, validation selection, or branch pruning.
7. **Observation:** log sample-weighted training objective, gradient norm and relative parameter-update norm, without adaptation. At both fixed checkpoints, compute full-fit common endpoint NMSE and logit KL for every cell. Only after *all* planned trajectories/checkpoints/failures are frozen, evaluate the 1,000 diagnostic images once in a single reporting phase for **both prespecified checkpoints of every cell**. Retain per-image predictions and teacher labels/logits; then expose diagnostic ground-truth labels for exact accuracy, paired disagreement counts and teacher drop. No intermediate diagnostic peeks or choice of a “best” checkpoint/width/loss. Report the entire matrix, including failures.

## Decision rules fixed before execution

Use the same centered endpoint NMSE definition as the pilot. The **primary mechanistic contrast** is the width8/MSE 1,024→2,048 budget effect; width, loss, and their interactions are prespecified secondary contrasts, all reported regardless of the primary result. Report per-seed contrasts; n=3 teacher seeds sharing images is not a population-level replication sample. The following are **descriptive evidence flags**, not significance tests or proof of an exclusive cause:

- **Budget sensitivity:** within a width/objective, 1,024→2,048 lowers both full-fit and diagnostic endpoint NMSE by ≥10% relative. It contributes to the task-utility gap only when diagnostic accuracy also rises ≥1 pp (10/1,000). Falling training error alone may be overfitting; a flat curve does not prove convergence.
- **Width sensitivity:** at the fixed 2,048 budget under endpoint MSE, width16 versus width8 lowers both fit and diagnostic NMSE by ≥10%, with ≥1 pp accuracy gain for a task-relevant effect. If width8's final budget contrast changes fit NMSE by <5%, label this “width benefit after apparent plateau,” **not proven insufficient expressivity**. Width also changes conditioning and compute. The KL cells reveal whether the width effect depends on the objective.
- **Feature/task alignment evidence:** at the same width and fixed 2,048 budget, KL versus MSE improves diagnostic accuracy ≥1 pp and lowers diagnostic logit KL ≥10%, while endpoint NMSE is equal or worse. That dissociation supports objective alignment as a contributor. If NMSE also improves, the result supports the alternative objective but does not isolate alignment. KL changes loss geometry/gradient scale as well; no uniquely causal claim from this one contrast.
- Report each flag by seed. A consistent three-seed direction is descriptive consistency; mixed directions, subthreshold contrasts, any missing cells, or both interventions helping remain **mixed/inconclusive**, not permission to tune. Twofold width cannot test the missing 9×9 receptive field. Twofold budget cannot exclude gains at much longer horizons. If no factor helps, “unresolved under tested changes,” not “capacity proven inadequate.”

## Minimum cost and bounds

Four trajectories × three teachers = **12 trajectories**, each 2,048 updates: **24,576 optimizer updates**, **3,072,000 fit-image presentations**, plus prespecified metrics. The second budget reuses each trajectory's checkpoint; it does not require additional fits. No teacher retraining, downloads, original-validation inference or official-test inference. At diagnostic reporting: eight checkpoints × 1,000 images × three teachers = 24,000 student-image predictions, plus teacher predictions. This is the minimum complete 2×2 width/objective design with a within-trajectory budget contrast for all three teachers; not a claim of globally minimal scientific cost.

**Why not only a budget extension?** A fresh width8/MSE trajectory per teacher would cost 6,144 updates / 768,000 fit-image presentations and answer the cheaper question “does doubling this budget help?” It is sufficient for that narrow question, but a negative result cannot distinguish capacity, loss alignment or optimizer limitations; a positive result cannot exclude their contributions. Continuing the original students is cheaper still but cannot create an unseen diagnostic split. Because the requested diagnosis covers all three mechanisms, this proposal fixes the four-cell design once rather than using diagnostic results to decide which additional intervention to try. Its incremental cost over the fresh budget-only contrast is 18,432 updates / 2,304,000 presentations. This is an explanation of the selected design, not authorization for a second/adaptive protocol.

Proposed cap: one four-thread worker, inter-op1, global science threads≤8, original teachers have priority; **2 h per teacher including all four trajectories, loading, metrics and downtime**, no cap extension, and no later than 2026-10-29 06:50 UTC. At cap, retain last atomic finite checkpoint and mark incomplete, without substituting a lower checkpoint as a final result. The maximum allocation is 6 h sequential wall time / 24 CPU-thread-hours across three teachers, not a predicted runtime. Actual wall-time feasibility for differentiated frozen-suffix KL is **not measured**; inherited pilot timings cannot establish it. Bound is a proposed maximum, not a launch authorization. No fees, CI or automatic retries.
