## Interpretation through G6c

The transfer map is explicitly **mixed**.

- G3: adapted transfer into ViT.
- G5: zero-shot transfer within the Transformer family to a controlled non-image encoder.
- G6: synthetic causal decoding requires bounded repair.
- G6b: natural English exposes a stronger autoregressive trajectory boundary.
- **G6c shows that teacher-forced output awareness alone is insufficient.** Adding logit KL preserves next-token likelihood almost perfectly but does not stabilize free-running trajectories on fresh seeds.

Together v23 and v24 narrow the likely failure mechanism. The problem is not simply that the compiler ignores output logits; it is that it is optimized primarily on prefixes from the data/teacher distribution while evaluation feeds back prefixes produced by the compiled model itself.

## Strong evaluation lesson

For autoregressive models, **teacher-forced likelihood is necessary but not sufficient**. A compiler can retain PPL at ~0.997 relative utility and still disagree on roughly 44% of rollout tokens over a 24-token horizon.

## Next highest-information tests

1. **Trajectory-aware / on-policy distillation:** train the fixed-capacity compiler against teacher outputs on prefixes generated by the compiler itself (bounded one-iteration dataset aggregation), then freeze and reconfirm on fresh seeds.
2. Small pretrained/subword LM on real text only after an autoregressive objective is fixed.
3. G4/G3b CIFAR-10 ResNet/ViT to map task difficulty outside language.
4. Attention-only vs MLP-only causal compilation to identify the sensitive decoder submodule.
