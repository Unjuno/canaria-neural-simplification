# Historical material index

The current repository surface intentionally excludes the older version-number research sequence from first-class navigation. Historical material remains preserved under `../archives/`.

## Current authority

For current interpretation, use:

- `../README.md`
- `../STATUS.md`
- `ANNOUNCEMENT_READINESS.md`
- `CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE.md`
- `INDEPENDENT_REREVIEW_2026-08-26.md`

Do not infer current status from archived version numbers, old “next experiment” text, or a historical release checklist.

## Where older material moved

- old `docs/history/` -> `../archives/research-history/docs/history/`
- old `docs/phases/` -> `../archives/research-history/docs/phases/`
- old versioned result directories -> `../archives/research-history/results/`
- old versioned phase runners -> `../archives/research-history/scripts/`
- old environment history -> `../archives/research-history/environment/`

Broad historical planning/status files such as the former `GENERALIZATION_ROADMAP.md`, `GENERALIZATION_STATUS.md`, `KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md`, `RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md`, and `NEXT_EXPERIMENTS_AUTONOMOUS.md` are under `../archives/research-history/legacy-docs/`.

Historical release/snapshot gate records are under `../archives/releases/`; the completed review handoff is under `../archives/reviews/`.

See `../archives/README.md` for the detailed migration map.

## Preservation rule

Historical protocols, outcome ledgers, scripts, and invalidated evidence are not rewritten to make them look consistent with later theory. Current documents explain corrections and claim boundaries around the preserved record.
