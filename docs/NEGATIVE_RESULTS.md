# Negative results and falsified explanations

Negative results are retained because they constrain the theory.

## Plain 8-block CNN trainability

The original plain 8-block architecture collapsed to chance-level performance in pilot seeds. Confirmatory work therefore moved to a residual 8-block network *before* simplification/Canary outcomes were inspected.

## Canary-local explanation

The blinded confirmatory map found strong simplification frequently in low-Canary spans. A model in which high Canary is necessary for simplification is incompatible with the confirmatory result.

## Strong Canary prediction

Adding Canary percentile to width only improved LOSO AUC by about 0.006 and the confidence interval crossed zero. Do not market the current Canary definition as a strong predictor.

## Pure post-shell location effect

When capacity and adapter class were matched between pre and post boundaries, the post location was not superior; in several interventions pre performed slightly better. Earlier post-shell advantage was confounded by available capacity/topology.

## Unlimited recursive recompilation

After an initial large collapse and repair, collapsing the repaired post-shell to a single linear or small hidden representation generally failed. The evidence is inconsistent with a simple rule that repeated compilation monotonically drives complexity to zero.

## Four/twelve scalar functions

Reducing precision is easy relative to reducing the number of scalar degrees of freedom. Direct top-K and dictionary experiments showed that 4 or 12 scalars were far from sufficient for the studied Conv3 replacement.

## Two-bit naive quantization

Naive global/tensor 2-bit quantization failed badly. Channelwise scale selection and calibration were necessary to make very low precision competitive.

## Unstructured sparsity storage

Sparse weights with explicit indices can be larger than a dense 3-bit tensor at comparable quality. Structured sparsity was required to obtain a better real storage frontier.

## Some sub-10-KB head designs

Low-rank and structured heads could preserve the head transformation itself but still fail whole-network utility due to seed-level interactions. A slightly larger core plus structured head ultimately gave the stable 9,926-byte solution.
